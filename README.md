# Ultimate-Scheduler
This is my one-file HTML Ultimate scheduling app. Throw it in a directory, "Ctrl-O" in your browser, open the scheduler.html file, and BAM, there it is, Dead Simple.
It's an attempt to replicate much of one of my favorite old-school scheduling apps: Above and Beyond by 1Soft (1soft.com).
A&B was wonderful for its wildly overcomplicated functionality, bizarre dialog boxes, and crazy help dialog boxes! If you loved over-organization of your life, A&B couldn't be beat.

This Ultimate scheduling app is meant to be GTD friendly, drag-and-drop easy, with color coded auto-fill tags and Urgency buttons.
There is PDF output of all views with a nice "Scratchpad" where you can put random notes that will output at the bottom of the PDF. It's made to be written in!

Shortcuts:
"n": New item
"Ctrl-Enter": save item

The Ultimate Scheduler doesn't draw a hard line between appointments (Hard start time) and todos (No hard start time). Anything can be dragged-and-dropped to and from the todo area (top of each daily column),
down to the daily time grid. You "dump" your life into todo's (using "n" and "Ctrl-Enter" make this sooo fast), and then you clarify them later and schedule them if you want. Everything drags-and-drops around converting to and from
appointment and todo as needed. The todo area is sortable.

Tags auto-color according to the first tag, and matching text pops up matching tags preventing misspellings and unwanted spelling variations.
Green, yellow and red dots correspond to low, medium and high urgency.
